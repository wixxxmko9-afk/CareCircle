Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-16

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.
  • Your family's information is visible only to the people in your family circle, and only as far as their role allows.
  • Your data is not end-to-end encrypted. It is stored in a Google Sheet that the person who runs CareCircle can open. Details below.
  • You can delete your account and data yourself, any time, in Settings.

Who we are

CareCircle is a small, independent project that helps families coordinate care for an older relative: medications, appointments, caregiver shifts, an emergency card and a family feed. "We" means the individual who runs CareCircle. You can reach us at carecircle.help.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

About you

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrambled hash. We can't read it.
  • Your role in the family (for example caregiver or viewer), and when you agreed to our Terms and this policy.

About the person receiving care

Entered by the caregivers: name, date of birth, blood type, weight, allergies, medical conditions, and insurance carrier, policy and group numbers. This is health information and is sensitive. Only enter it if you have the person's permission or legal authority to do so (see the Terms).

Care records

  • Emergency contacts: names and phone numbers.
  • Medications: names, doses, times, reasons, pharmacy, end dates, and which doses were marked taken, by whom and when.
  • Appointments and caregiver shifts.
  • Family feed: posts, comments and reactions.
  • Activity history: who changed what and when, including when an emergency-card link is created, opened or turned off.
  • Invitations: the name and email of people you invite, their role and the invite's status.

Email reminders (optional, off by default)

If you turn on email reminders in Settings, we email you about your family's medications and appointments: medication names, doses and times, missed-dose alerts, and appointment details. We store your choices, your family's timezone, and a log of which reminders were already sent (internal ids and times only, with no names or medicines). These emails pass through Google's Gmail and your own email provider and are not end-to-end encrypted, so anyone who can read your inbox can read them. Turn the reminders off at any time in Settings.

Technical data

  • When you log in, a login token is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. The server stores only a hash of it, and it expires after 30 days. Logging out ends it immediately.
  • Your display preferences (such as large text and timezone) are kept in your browser.
  • We don't use cookies or analytics tools. Like any website, Google sees your device's IP address when your device loads the app's fonts or talks to our Google-hosted backend.

How we use it

  • To provide the app's features to you and your family.
  • To send you emails you need: invitations and password reset codes, and the reminders you choose to turn on.
  • To keep accounts secure, for example by limiting repeated wrong-password attempts.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your data is stored in Google's cloud. Google chooses the data-centre location, which may be outside your country.

What we do: passwords are hashed with PBKDF2; login tokens are stored hashed; everything travels over HTTPS; the server checks your role on every change; wrong-password attempts are limited; and shared links use long random secrets that we store only as hashes.

Honest limits

  • Your data is not end-to-end encrypted. The person who runs CareCircle, and Google as the host, can technically read it.
  • We don't yet offer email verification or two-step login, so choose a strong password you don't use anywhere else.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.
  • In-app reminders only work while the app is open on your device. Optional email reminders depend on Google and your email provider and can be late or fail. Don't rely on either as your only reminder.

How long we keep it

  • Your information stays until you delete it or your account.
  • Login sessions expire after 30 days. Shared emergency-card links expire after 24 hours.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from the app's spreadsheet straight away. Copies may remain in Google's backups or the spreadsheet's version history for a limited time until Google's retention periods end. Emails already sent can't be recalled.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything is visible in the app. Caregivers can edit medications, the emergency card and appointments.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account (full steps and what is erased: how to delete your account). If you're a family member, this removes you, your posts, comments and reactions. Lines about you in the activity history become "Former member". If you're the primary caregiver, it deletes the whole family and every record in it, and everyone loses access.
  • Ask us: email carecircle.help.app@gmail.com to get a copy of your information, correct it, or have it deleted. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: by deleting your account.

Children

CareCircle is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change, we'll update the date above and ask you to agree again the next time you open the app.
