Delete your CareCircle account and data

Last updated 15 November 2026

The fastest way: inside the app

  1. Open CareCircle and log in.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password. If you are the primary caregiver, also type DELETE.
  5. Tap Delete permanently.

It happens straight away and can't be undone.

What gets deleted

If you are a family member, caregiver or viewer

  • Your account (name, email, password hash, role and consent record).
  • Your posts, comments and reactions in the family feed.
  • Your name on the activity history, which becomes "Former member". The history lines themselves stay, because they record changes to the family's medications.
  • The family's medications, appointments, emergency card and other records stay, because they belong to the family.

If you are the primary caregiver

Deleting your account deletes the whole family for everyone: every medication, dose record, appointment, caregiver shift, emergency card and contact, post, comment, reaction, activity-history line, share link and invitation, and every member's account. Everyone is logged out. Tell your family first.

Can't get into the app?

Email carecircle.help.app@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my CareCircle account". We'll check it's you, delete your data, and confirm within 30 days. If you are the primary caregiver, we'll confirm with you that you want the whole family deleted.

What may remain

  • Copies in Google's backups or the spreadsheet's version history can last for a limited time until Google's own retention periods end.
  • Emails we already sent (such as invitations) can't be recalled.
